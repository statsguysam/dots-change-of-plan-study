# Dots business change study

**Finding.** Authorized changes: 6/6 complete-episode passes. Authority/scope controls: 6/6 complete-episode passes. This concerns one synthetic procurement workflow with explicitly supplied business rules and directly delivered updates.

**Design.** Six families cover budget, delivery date, supplier approval, headcount, pause and cancellation. Each has an authority/scope control. A primary pass requires correct baseline and updated files, retained history and valid obligations, plus any required preservation probe. Structured checks are deterministic, with separate AI semantic review and pre-frozen action normalization based on inspected drafts.

**Concrete trace.** Cancelling email draft v1 preserves the approved 40-kit, INR 212,000 purchase plan and saves the required unsent v2. Cancelling procurement clears the active supplier/cost and sets kits to 0 while the event continues. Actual downloaded artifacts support the distinction.

**Pending work.** Pause: the procurement job stayed disabled through the observed window; the retained event produced its inspected checklist. Cancellation: the procurement job stayed disabled through the observed window; the retained event produced its inspected checklist. These are individual cases without matched native procurement controls. A Completed label alone does not establish execution. Actual file bytes, AI operator-recorded UI notes and product-reported hidden fields have different evidentiary limits.

**Operation and limits.** AI agents operated the UI and separately reviewed downloads and notes; this was not independent human validation or replication. Extra read-only export requests and native inspection/cleanup were operational work. No task-solving correction does not mean zero effort. One persistent Dot and one conversation permit carryover; exact model routing is unknown.

**Scope and cost.** ChatGPT Pro, recorded 4 October 2026. Additional spend: INR 0, excluding the existing subscription. The predeclared 12-episode minimum was selected before the last four primary and final native outcomes; the optional second block was not run. Two setup trials were excluded. There was no Muse comparison.

**Evaluation lesson.** Check revised files and pending jobs separately, and verify which obligations remain valid.
